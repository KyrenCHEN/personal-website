Our paper *The First Seasonal Green View Index Mapping Dataset across Chinese cities powered by deep learning* has been published in **Scientific Data** (12(1), 1356). The study presents the first seasonal Green View Index (GVI) mapping dataset across Chinese cities, produced with deep learning.
